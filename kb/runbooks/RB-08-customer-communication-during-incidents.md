# Customer communication during incidents

## Symptoms

*RB-08 · Customer communication during incidents · updated 2026-06-10*
An incident is affecting customers and support needs consistent wording.

## Rules for internal responders

*RB-08 · Customer communication during incidents · updated 2026-06-10*
1. Say what customers experience, not what broke inside. Never name internal services, clusters or tools.
2. Do not state a root cause until the incident is resolved and reviewed.
3. Never promise a refund, a credit or a time to fix. Refund decisions belong to Billing.
4. Give an update time and keep it.
5. Be plain and kind. Apologise once, clearly.

## Checks (in order)

*RB-08 · Customer communication during incidents · updated 2026-06-10*
Before sending anything, check the severity (RB-07), the current status from the on-call engineer, and the approved wording below.

## Approved wording

*RB-08 · Customer communication during incidents · updated 2026-06-10*
- Start of incident: "Some customers are having trouble paying invoices. We are working on it and will update you by <time>."
- Resolved: "Payments are working again. If a payment looks wrong on your account, reply here and our billing team will review it."

## Actions that need approval

*RB-08 · Customer communication during incidents · updated 2026-06-10*
Any message to all customers, or a status page change, needs approval from the platform owner.

## When to escalate

*RB-08 · Customer communication during incidents · updated 2026-06-10*
Customers report being charged twice. Hand those cases to Billing the same day, using the guidance customers see in the product docs.
