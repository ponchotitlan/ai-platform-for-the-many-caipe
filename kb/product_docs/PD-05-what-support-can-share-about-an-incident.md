# What support can share about an incident

## Overview
When something is wrong with LedgerLite, customers ask what happened. This page says what support can and cannot say.

## What you can say
- That some customers are having trouble paying invoices (or whatever the visible problem is).
- That the team is working on it.
- When the next update will come, if you were given a time.
- That unpaid invoices stay open while the problem lasts.
- That customers who see a payment problem on their account can contact support, and Billing will review it.

## What you cannot say
- The technical cause, or the names of internal systems, servers or tools.
- Who made a change or when a release happened.
- A promised time for the fix.
- A promise of a refund or credit.

## Example
Customer: "What caused the problem?"
Answer: "Some customers are having trouble paying invoices right now. Our team is working on it and I'll share an update as soon as there is one. Your invoice stays open, so nothing is lost. I'm sorry for the trouble."

## Getting help
If you are unsure what to say, ask the support lead before replying.
