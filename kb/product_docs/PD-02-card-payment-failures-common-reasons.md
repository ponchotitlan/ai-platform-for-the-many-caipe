# Card payment failures: common reasons

## Overview

*PD-02 · Card payment failures: common reasons · updated 2026-05-05*
A card payment can fail for reasons on the card side or on our side. This page lists the common ones and what to try.

## Common reasons

*PD-02 · Card payment failures: common reasons · updated 2026-05-05*
- **Card declined by the bank**: not enough funds, a spending limit, or the bank blocked an unusual payment. Ask the customer to contact their bank or try another card.
- **Wrong card details**: number, expiry date or security code entered incorrectly.
- **Card not enabled for online payments**: some cards need this turned on in the bank app.
- **Temporary problem on our side**: the payment page can spin and then show "something went wrong". The payment was not completed. It is safe to try again later.

## What support can tell customers

*PD-02 · Card payment failures: common reasons · updated 2026-05-05*
- Check the card details and try again in a few minutes.
- Try another card, or pay later: invoices stay open.
- If the page showed an error, check that the invoice is not already marked Paid or Processing before trying again.

## What to avoid

*PD-02 · Card payment failures: common reasons · updated 2026-05-05*
- Do not tell the customer a specific cause for a problem on our side.
- Do not promise a time when it will work again.

## Getting help

*PD-02 · Card payment failures: common reasons · updated 2026-05-05*
If it keeps failing after two tries, collect the invoice number and the time of the attempt and pass it to support.
