# Customer data handling for support

## Overview

*PD-06 · Customer data handling for support · updated 2026-03-30*
Customer account data is personal data. This page sets the rules for how support handles it.

## Rules

*PD-06 · Customer data handling for support · updated 2026-03-30*
- Share account details (invoices, amounts, card digits) only with the verified account owner, through the verified channel.
- Never paste raw invoice or customer records into chats, tickets or messages to other people.
- Do not look up another customer's data to compare cases.
- Do not read out full card numbers. Only the last four digits may be used.
- Questions about which invoices were affected by a problem go through the verified channel, one account at a time.

## When someone asks "which invoices were affected on my account?"

*PD-06 · Customer data handling for support · updated 2026-03-30*
Support cannot read out account data in an unverified chat. Say so politely, then offer the route: the customer signs in and checks the invoice list, or support sends a summary through the verified account contact after the account owner is confirmed.

## Escalation

*PD-06 · Customer data handling for support · updated 2026-03-30*
If a request seems to involve someone else's data, or you think data was exposed, tell the privacy team the same day.

## Suggested wording

*PD-06 · Customer data handling for support · updated 2026-03-30*
"For your security I can't share account details here. If you sign in and open your invoice list, you can see each invoice's state. I can also send a summary to the verified contact on your account."
