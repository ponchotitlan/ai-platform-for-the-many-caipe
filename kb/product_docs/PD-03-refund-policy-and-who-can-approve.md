# Refund policy and who can approve

## Overview
Refunds and credits are decided by the Billing team. Support agents cannot approve or promise them.

## Policy
- Refund requests are reviewed by Billing, usually within 5 business days.
- A refund is only approved after Billing checks the payment record.
- A duplicate charge for the same invoice is normally reversed after Billing review. See the page on duplicate charges.
- Credits and goodwill gestures are decided by Billing and the account owner.

## What support can do
- Collect the invoice number, the amount, the date and the last four digits of the card.
- Open a request with Billing and tell the customer it has been sent for review.
- Explain that Billing will contact them with the outcome.

## What support must not do
- Promise a refund, an amount or a date.
- Say that a refund is "approved" or "on its way".
- Process a refund directly.

## Suggested wording
"I'm sorry about this. I have sent your request to our billing team, who will review it and contact you. I can't confirm a refund myself."
