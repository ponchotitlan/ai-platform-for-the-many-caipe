# Duplicate charges: what to tell customers

## Overview

*PD-04 · Duplicate charges: what to tell customers · updated 2026-04-22*
A customer may see two charges for one invoice, often after a payment page showed an error and they tried again. This page says what to tell them.

## What is going on

*PD-04 · Duplicate charges: what to tell customers · updated 2026-04-22*
A payment can be started twice when the first attempt appears to fail. Often one of the two charges is a temporary authorisation that the bank releases. If both were completed, Billing reverses the extra one.

## What to tell the customer

*PD-04 · Duplicate charges: what to tell customers · updated 2026-04-22*
1. Apologise for the trouble.
2. Explain that Billing will review the account and the payments.
3. If a duplicate charge is confirmed, it is typically reversed within 5 business days after Billing review. Banks can take a little longer to show it on a statement.
4. Ask them not to pay the same invoice again until the review is done.

## What support does

*PD-04 · Duplicate charges: what to tell customers · updated 2026-04-22*
- Collect the invoice number, the dates and amounts of both charges, and the last four digits of the card.
- Send the case to Billing (see the refund policy page).

## What to avoid

*PD-04 · Duplicate charges: what to tell customers · updated 2026-04-22*
- Do not promise a refund or a date. Say "typically" and "after review".
- Do not name internal systems or give a cause.

## Suggested wording

*PD-04 · Duplicate charges: what to tell customers · updated 2026-04-22*
"I'm sorry about the double charge. I've sent this to our billing team to review. If a duplicate is confirmed, it is typically reversed within 5 business days after their review. Please don't pay the invoice again meanwhile."
